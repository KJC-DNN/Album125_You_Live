# Album 125 — You Live 

You Live - நீர் வாழ்க

Letters of Lament and Light — Album 125

Seven prayer letters of glory, presence, and waiting. Morning praise, birthday gift, soft mercy, Maranatha, and rain. Tamil original and English companion. Sense kept.

2. Birthday Gift

You live. He alone remains. Come, Lord Jesus.
